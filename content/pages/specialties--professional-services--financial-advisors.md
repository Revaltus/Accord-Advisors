## Outsourced Accounting Built for Financial Advisory Practices

Financial advisors build their practices on trust, precision, and a fee structure that rarely looks like anyone else's. AUM billing, trail commissions, and revenue-sharing arrangements make bookkeeping for an RIA a different animal than bookkeeping for a retail business, and most bookkeepers have never seen a custodial statement up close. Accord Advisors works alongside healthcare providers, professional service providers, and independent financial practices nationwide, including firms in Bloomington, Avon, and the greater Indianapolis area of Indiana, building the financial infrastructure advisors need to run a compliant, scalable firm.

Our team provides outsourced accounting, payroll processing, and CFO-level financial guidance for independent advisors and RIA owners who need their books handled by people who understand fee-based revenue, not just retail sales.

## Why Financial Advisors Need Their Own Trusted Accounting Partner

There's an irony built into this profession. Advisors spend their days managing other people's money, projecting growth, and flagging risk, yet their own back office often runs on whatever bookkeeping habit they picked up when the firm had three clients instead of three hundred. Reconciliations slip. Payroll gets handled between client meetings. Revenue reports lag a quarter behind reality.

Handing the books to a dedicated accounting team changes that. Advisors get back the hours they were spending on data entry and spreadsheet cleanup, and they get a clearer read on how the practice is actually performing. That clarity matters when a firm is deciding whether to hire another advisor, add a support role, or hold steady for another year.

Accord Advisors takes the accounting function off an advisor's plate entirely, so the advisor's calendar goes back to client meetings, prospecting, and portfolio work instead of chasing down a missing deposit.

## Fixed Monthly Fees, No Surprises

Hourly billing punishes the exact behavior a growing advisory firm needs most: asking questions, requesting a deeper look at a report, or getting an accountant on the phone before a big decision. Accord Advisors charges a fixed monthly fee instead, set in advance and scoped to the firm's size and service needs.

- Predictable monthly cost that fits into the firm's own budget
- No hourly clock running when an advisor calls with a question
- Pricing set upfront, reviewed as the practice grows
- One fee covers bookkeeping, payroll, and advisory support together

Advisors can estimate their own cost before committing using the [pricing calculator](/pricing-calculator), and adjust services as the practice adds staff or assets under management.

## Secure, Cloud-Based Platform for Real-Time Financial Clarity

Financial data deserves the same security standard advisors already apply to client accounts. Accord Advisors gives every client firm-wide access to a secure online platform where financial statements, payroll records, and reports live in one place, updated on a monthly cycle instead of arriving as a year-end surprise.
