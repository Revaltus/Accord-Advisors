## Outsourced Accounting Built for Consulting Firms

Consulting firms don't sell products off a shelf. Revenue comes from project fees, monthly retainers, and billable hours that shift with client demand, and the accounting behind that model looks nothing like a typical small business ledger. Accord Advisors works as the outsourced accounting department for consulting and professional services firms nationwide, including Bloomington, Avon, and the Indianapolis area of Indiana, tracking project profitability, retainer revenue, and utilization the way a firm principal actually thinks about the business. Our CPA-lead team handles work-in-progress, unbilled time, and client contracts as they move through your books, all visible on a secure online platform that stays current day to day. Instead of hiring a full-time controller or stitching together software on your own, you get one accounting relationship covering the daily bookkeeping and the bigger financial questions behind it.

## Bookkeeping and Cash Flow Management That Keeps You in Control

Project-based work creates a timing problem: you might deliver the work in March, invoice in April, and get paid in June, all while payroll and overhead bills keep coming on schedule. Accord Advisors handles the bookkeeping that keeps that gap from becoming a crisis. Accounts get reconciled, project expenses get categorized correctly, and accounts receivable gets managed so invoices go out on time and unpaid balances don't slip through the cracks. Retainer clients are tracked separately from project work, so you can see at a glance which revenue is recurring and which depends on the next engagement closing. Cash flow reports show what's actually available to spend, not just what's sitting in the bank account waiting to cover next month's payroll or subcontractor payments. All of it lives on a secure online platform, so you can check your firm's financial position from a laptop between client meetings instead of waiting on a monthly binder or a callback from a bookkeeper. Whether your firm is based in Bloomington, Avon, Indianapolis, or anywhere else in the country, you stay informed about where the firm stands. We stay on top of the details that get it there.

Should the next engagement be staffed with a new hire or a subcontractor? Is this the year to open a second office or bring on a partner? Accord Advisors brings that level of financial analysis to consulting firms nationwide that aren't ready to carry a full-time CFO on payroll. Succession and ownership transition planning fall into this same conversation for firms thinking years ahead rather than just next quarter. ## Proactive Tax Planning for Consultants

Consulting income rarely arrives in even amounts, which makes quarterly estimated tax payments one of the more painful parts of running a firm. Underpay and you owe penalties. Overpay and you've tied up cash that could have covered payroll or funded new equipment. Accord Advisors builds a tax plan around your actual income pattern instead of a generic estimate, so quarterly payments track what the firm is really earning. Entity structure decisions, S corporation elections, retirement plan contributions, and deductions specific to project-based service firms get worked through as well, questions that come up constantly for principals managing 1099 income alongside W-2 payroll for staff. Tax planning happens throughout the year rather than in a scramble every April, so decisions about equipment purchases, hiring, or year-end bonuses get made with the tax impact already factored in. Fewer surprises at filing time, more of what the firm earns stays in the firm. Firms just getting their structure in place can start with [Business Foundation Services](/services/business-foundation-services) before tax planning even begins.
