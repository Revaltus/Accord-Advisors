## Understanding the financial pressures optometry practices face

Optometry is a retail business and a medical practice running side by side, and that combination creates pressure points that don't show up in a standard CPA's playbook.

- Frame and lens inventory that ties up working capital and needs regular turnover analysis, not a once-a-year count
- Insurance reimbursement timing that leaves gaps between the exam and the cash actually landing in your account
- Staffing costs that swing with patient volume, from opticians and technicians to front-desk coverage
- Owner compensation questions that get tangled up with the practice's real profitability
- The need for monthly, not annual, visibility into how the practice actually performed

Without monthly clarity, staffing and purchasing decisions get made on instinct instead of numbers. That's an expensive way to run a practice.
