- Vendor and accounts payable management, so media buys and subscriptions get paid without you tracking due dates
- Clean, categorized records delivered through a secure online portal you can check anytime, from anywhere

This isn't a once-a-year cleanup service. It's ongoing support that keeps your financials accurate month over month, which matters when a client, lender, or potential buyer asks to see your numbers.

## Managing Project-Based Cash Flow

Agency cash flow rarely moves in a straight line. Retainer clients pay on schedule, but project work often means invoicing at kickoff, at milestones, and again at delivery, with 30 or 60 days between the invoice and the deposit. Add in a slow-paying client or two, and payroll week can feel like a gamble even when the agency is profitable on paper.

Accord Advisors builds cash flow forecasts around how your agency actually bills, not a generic template. We track receivables by client and project, flag payment patterns that are trending slower, and help you plan payroll, contractor payments, and tax obligations around real cash timing instead of guesswork. That means fewer surprises when a big client pushes payment by three weeks, and more confidence heading into slower months. For agencies carrying a mix of retainers and one-off projects, this kind of visibility is often the difference between reacting to cash gaps and planning around them.

## CFO-Level Insight for Creative Leaders

Knowing your agency made money last quarter isn't the same as knowing which clients, projects, or service lines actually drove that profit. Agencies frequently discover that a high-revenue account is barely breaking even once staff hours and overhead are factored in, while a smaller retainer is quietly one of the most profitable relationships on the books.

Accord Advisors provides profitability analysis by client and project, margin tracking, and growth planning support, the kind of insight a full-time CFO would deliver, without the six-figure salary. You get dashboards and reports built around the numbers that actually drive agency decisions: utilization, project margin, and client concentration risk. That's strategic guidance for pricing new business, staffing up, or deciding which accounts are worth keeping, delivered as part of your fixed monthly fee rather than billed as a separate consulting engagement.
