## Personal tax help that goes beyond filing a return

You own a practice or run a service business, and your personal return touches your K-1, your distributions, and your retirement contributions. We get it. Accord Advisors takes the year-round tax work off your plate, so you are not scrambling in April or guessing in July.

<!-- block: checklist-section | variant: standalone -->
## Common personal tax problems and how we fix them

- **Problem:** One preparer for the business, another for the personal return, and neither talks to the other. **Fix:** The same advisor who handles your [outsourced accounting](/services/outsourced-accounting) or [business foundation services](/services/business-foundation-services) prepares your 1040.
- **Problem:** A home sale, bonus, or retirement account question comes up mid-year with no one to call. **Fix:** Year-round tax planning support, with no hourly meter running.
- **Problem:** Estimated payments feel like guesswork. **Fix:** We calculate them so you know what to send the IRS each quarter.
- **Problem:** A practice sale or retirement changes your tax picture. **Fix:** Planning ahead of time through [succession planning](/services/succession-planning).
- **Problem:** Returns you sign without understanding. **Fix:** Plain-language explanations of what changed and why, before you sign.

<!-- block: content-split | variant: image-left | image: secure-client-portal-laptop.jpg | alt: "Client uploading tax documents through a secure online portal on a laptop" | query: "person using laptop online document upload" -->
## Fixed fees and a secure client portal

Personal tax preparation and planning is priced as a fixed monthly or annual fee, agreed to upfront. You know the cost before work starts and can call whenever a question comes up.

- Upload W-2s, 1099s, and closing statements to a secure online portal as they arrive
- Federal and Indiana returns reviewed by a CPA before filing
- Deduction and compliance guidance throughout the year

## Frequently Asked Questions About Personal income tax preparation and planning

**Q: Does Accord Advisors prepare personal tax returns for business owners?**
A: Yes. Most individual clients also own a practice or service business, so personal returns are coordinated with business accounting by the same advisor. This keeps K-1s, distributions, and retirement contributions aligned across both returns instead of handled separately.

**Q: How much does personal income tax preparation cost?**
A: Accord Advisors uses fixed monthly or annual fees agreed to upfront, not hourly billing. You will know the cost before work begins and can ask questions throughout the year without worrying about a running meter.

**Q: Do you serve clients outside Bloomington and Indianapolis?**
A: Yes. Accord Advisors serves individuals and business owners nationwide. Documents move through a secure online portal, so clients across the country work with us just as closely as those in Bloomington, Avon and the Indianapolis area. See our [locations](/locations) for details.

**Q: What if my tax situation involves selling a business or retiring?**
A: These are the moments personal tax planning matters most. We guide clients through practice sales, succession transitions, and retirement distributions well before filing, so the tax impact is planned for rather than discovered. Learn more about [succession planning](/services/succession-planning).

**Q: Do you work with optometrists, dentists, and medical professionals?**
A: Yes. Many individual clients are optometry practice shareholders, physicians, dentists, and service business owners. Optometrists and [medical professionals](/industries/medical-professionals) often come through industry networks and value having one advisor see both their practice and their household return.

**Q: Why does personal tax preparation work better year-round?**
A: A return prepared once a year, without context, misses decisions made months earlier. Questions about a home sale in July, a retirement account in October, or a bonus in January are easier to answer when we are already planning next year's return.

**Q: How do I send my tax documents?**
A: You upload them through a secure online client portal instead of email attachments or paper drop-offs. Add a W-2, a 1099, or a closing statement whenever it arrives, and it is available to us immediately, with no faxing or waiting for an office to reopen.

**Q: How does your approach differ from a seasonal tax preparer?**
A: A seasonal storefront preparer handles a return and moves on. Our CPA leads tax planning alongside outsourced accounting and business foundation clients, so the advisor who sees your monthly financials also prepares your 1040. Visit [team expertise](/team-expertise) or [why Accord Advisors](/why-accordadvisors), or see [getting started](/getting-started).
