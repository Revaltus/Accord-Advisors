- **Books fall behind:** Monthly reconciliations, general ledger upkeep and month-end close keep your numbers current.
- **Payroll eats your time:** Payroll, withholdings, payroll tax filings, W-2s and 1099s run through the same team that keeps your books.
- **Vendor bills stack up:** We code and review each bill, you approve with one click, and every payment lands in your books.
- **Tax season is a scramble:** CPA-led business tax is built on books that are already organized.
- **An IRS notice arrives:** A CPA who knows your filings reads it, checks your records and responds for you.
- **Church funds need tracking:** Fund accounting covers restricted and unrestricted funds, with board and treasurer reporting.
- **Hourly billing makes you hesitate to call:** A fixed monthly fee means you can ask freely.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What does outsourced accounting include?**
A: Accord Advisors' outsourced accounting covers bookkeeping and financial reporting, business tax, payroll, bill pay and vendor management, and IRS notice protection. One team handles all of it for a fixed monthly fee, so you are not piecing services together between a bookkeeper, a payroll provider and a tax preparer.

**Q: How is pricing structured for outsourced accounting services?**
A: Pricing is a fixed monthly fee, set after reviewing your practice or business size, transaction volume and reporting needs. There is no hourly billing, so you can email a question or request a report without watching a clock run against your invoice. You budget for it like rent or payroll.

**Q: What does bookkeeping and financial reporting cover?**
A: Bookkeeping includes bank and credit card reconciliations, general ledger maintenance, accounts payable and receivable tracking, and month-end close. Once your books are closed, you receive financial reports showing where revenue, expenses and cash stand, rather than entries waiting to be cleaned up at tax time.

**Q: What payroll tasks do you handle?**
A: Payroll runs on your schedule: weekly, biweekly or semi-monthly. The team calculates federal, state and local withholdings, files quarterly and annual payroll tax returns, sets up direct deposit, onboards new hires including state new-hire reporting, and prepares year-end W-2s and 1099s. Everything connects to your general ledger, so nothing is entered twice.

**Q: How does bill pay and vendor management work?**
A: Vendors send invoices to Accord Advisors, or you forward what reaches your inbox. Each bill is logged, checked for accuracy and coded to the right category, with price increases or duplicates flagged. You approve payments online, we send them by ACH, check or card, and each payment appears in your monthly financials.

**Q: What happens if I receive an IRS notice?**
A: A CPA who already knows your filings reads the notice line by line and checks it against your returns, payroll records or bookkeeping data. With proper authorization, the CPA communicates with the IRS for you, then drafts and files the response by the deadline. You review and approve it, so you never face the IRS alone.

**Q: Does Accord Advisors work with healthcare practices and medical professionals?**
A: Yes. The firm has deep expertise serving optometrists, dentists and medical professionals nationwide, including Bloomington, Avon and the greater Indianapolis area of Indiana. That includes insurance reimbursement timing, equipment financing cycles, staffing costs and the cash flow swings that come with adding a new provider.

**Q: Do you serve service-based businesses, professional service providers and churches?**
A: Yes. Accord Advisors works with service-based businesses, professional service providers and churches nationwide. Church clients get fund accounting with restricted and unrestricted fund tracking and board and treasurer reporting. Clients in Bloomington, Avon and Indianapolis are served alongside clients in other states under the same fixed-fee model.

**Q: How do I access my financial reports?**
A: Clients use a secure, cloud-based platform to view current financial reports, upload and retrieve documents, and give their accountant, bookkeeper and tax preparer access to the same live numbers. It works from a laptop or phone, and it keeps a dated, organized record of statements for lending, audits or ownership changes.
